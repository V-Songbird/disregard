What happens to what you paste here

How file review, scoring and prompt copying use your text.

When you paste or choose an instruction file, its preview is parsed in your browser. Previewing does not send the file for scoring. When you start analysis, eligible excerpts are sent individually to our scoring endpoint hosted on Cloudflare. Excerpts that pass the server's language screen are forwarded to TypeSafe AI, Inc. (United States) to be scored. In the one-rule mode, the submitted rule follows the same path. We are the data controller; TypeSafe is our processor.

TypeSafe's customer agreement excludes model training on submitted data without our prior consent. TypeSafe may derive telemetry from the text and process it to monitor abuse and comply with the law, including after our account ends. Its data processing addendum describes its role and obligations for personal data.

Cloudflare hosts the service and handles incoming requests. The scoring endpoint uses the client IP address supplied by Cloudflare to limit requests. People sharing an IP address share that allowance.

The file, source ranges and completed results stay in the current page so you can review them. Referenced files and links are not fetched automatically. We do not use submitted rules to train anything.

Your selected interface language is saved in this browser's local storage when available, so it can be restored on your next visit. Submitted rules, file contents, and scoring results are not saved there by the application.

The refactoring prompt is assembled in your browser from the scored excerpts, findings and coverage information. Generating or copying it makes no additional scoring request. Copying writes it to your clipboard; if that fails, you can copy it manually. Pasting it into another AI agent transfers the included text to the service you choose.

Do not paste secrets, credentials, or anyone's personal data. Rules often contain file paths, internal project names, or example emails — strip them first.

Results are a check on how a rule is written. They do not predict whether an AI will follow it.
