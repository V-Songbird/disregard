What happens to what you paste here

How file review, scoring and prompt copying use your text.

When you paste or choose an instruction file, its preview is parsed in your browser. Previewing does not send the file for scoring. When you start analysis, eligible excerpts are sent individually to TypeSafe AI, Inc. (United States) to be scored. In the one-rule mode, the submitted rule is sent instead. We are the data controller; TypeSafe is our processor.

TypeSafe will not use your text to train AI models. It may keep derived telemetry, and may process the text to monitor abuse and to comply with the law, including after our account ends.

The file, source ranges and completed results stay in the current page so you can review them. Referenced files and links are not fetched automatically. We do not use submitted rules to train anything.

The refactoring prompt is assembled in your browser from the scored excerpts, findings and coverage information. Generating or copying it makes no additional scoring request. Copying writes it to your clipboard; if that fails, you can copy it manually. Pasting it into another AI agent transfers the included text to the service you choose.

Do not paste secrets, credentials, or anyone's personal data. Rules often contain file paths, internal project names, or example emails — strip them first.

Results are a check on how a rule is written. They do not predict whether an AI will follow it.
